Step-by-Step Assembly Assistant

Click through the structural phases below. Keep track of your assembly build progress by checking off tasks in the interactive checklist widget.

Phase 1: Base & Leg Frame Setup

Easy
  1. Screw the four 3D-printed Feet to the workbench surface.
  2. Insert vertical steel pipe legs into the feet and lock them with Corner Leg Locks.
  3. Slide the Corner Bottoms onto the leg pipe tops.
  4. Diagonally measure corner-to-corner with a tape measure. Adjust corner feet until diagonals are within 1mm of each other.
🔧 Phase 1 Progress Checklist
💡 Leveling Tip: Do not fully tighten leg-lock clamping screws initially. Ensure all four corner bases sit at a level plane relative to the table surface first!

Phase 2: Carriage Trucks & Rail Squaring

Medium
  1. Press-fit the 608RS ball bearings into the Trucks using M8x40mm bolts.
  2. Snug the locknuts up gently (about 7 in/lbs of torque).
  3. Perform the Tilt Test: Tip the outer frame pipe at a 45-degree angle. The Trucks should slide slowly and smoothly from end to end without jamming.
🔧 Phase 2 Progress Checklist
⚠️ Caution: Using heavy socket wrenches can easily crush the 3D-printed parts. Tighten until the bolt heads make contact, then add only 1/4 of a turn.

Phase 3: GT2 Pulleys & Stepper Motors

Medium
  1. Mount the 4 NEMA 17 stepper motors onto the carriage trucks using M3x10mm screws.
  2. Position the GT2 Pulleys onto the motor shafts.
  3. Use the Wire Darryl printed spacer under the pulley to set the height relative to the idlers perfectly.
  4. Add a drop of threadlocker (Loctite) and tighten the grub screw directly against the flat of the motor shaft.
🔧 Phase 3 Progress Checklist
💡 Maintenance Tip: Loose pulleys are the number one cause of skipped steps and erratic alignment errors on custom CNC builds. Always use threadlocker on grub screws!

Phase 4: Gantry & Corner Tops

Hard
  1. Slip the center Core Carriage block onto the intersecting X and Y axis gantry pipes.
  2. Assemble X and Y axis tubes into the corner bases.
  3. Slide the Corner Tops over the pipes and secure them using M5 screws.
  4. Tension X and Y gantry timing belts, anchoring them securely into the upper and lower belt mounts.
🔧 Phase 4 Progress Checklist
📌 Note: Verify that the gantry belts slide with an even, springy tension. Do not over-tension as this will stress the motor shafts and cause binding.

Phase 5: Laser Module, Board & Wiring

Hard
  1. Mount the 5.5W Diode Laser Module onto the Z-axis Upper Tool Plate.
  2. Mount your Arduino UNO and CNC Shield inside an enclosure.
  3. Wire the stepper motors (dual X, dual Y) to driver outputs, ensuring matching polarity.
  4. Connect the laser TTL/PWM control pin to the standard spin-enable pin on the CNC Shield.
  5. Power on the SMPS and flash the Arduino with **GRBL v1.1**.
🔧 Phase 5 Progress Checklist
⚡ Safety First: Laser modules produce intense radiation. Always wear certified safety goggles before testing the laser, and ensure proper forced air ventilation is running!
